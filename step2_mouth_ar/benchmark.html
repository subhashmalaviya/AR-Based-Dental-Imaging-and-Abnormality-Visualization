Model & target comparisons

Run identical native image crops or sampled video frames through multiple checkpoints. Teeth and gums have separate scores. Unlabelled frames measure temporal change only.

1. Checkpoints

Learned U-Net and local IOP are selected for direct comparison. No model upload is needed. The historical U-Net remains optional. These models segment teeth only.

U-Net: public/models/tooth_seg.onnx (already included). IOP: ../IOP_teeth_segmentation/weights/maskrcnn.pt with its view classifier and post-processing.

IOP loads automatically on first comparison. Run this page on the computer containing your IOP project; CPU inference may take several seconds per frame.

Optional: add another ONNX checkpoint

Supports ToothNet model cards and RGB dental SegFormer cards. SegFormer cards declare toothClasses and optional gumClasses. No remote uploads.

2. Images or video sequences

Supports your patient contour JSON and saved editor labels. Uses the whole photo or the exact crop saved in your labels, up to 32 MP for photo benchmarking.

Or sample a video directly. A fixed mouth crop gives every checkpoint the same input. Leave width/height at 0 for the full frame. U-Net performs best on a close mouth crop.

Frame GT must match the video hash, timestamp and crop. Existing tooth-labels/1 files and dental-targets/1 teeth/gum masks are supported. Missing target annotations stay unlabelled.

No evaluation samples selected.
