Each tooth has its own 4×4 transform (position, rotation, scale) and is projected through a pinhole camera — not a scaled 2D sprite. Tracked: head orientation, from MediaPipe's facial transformation matrix. Measured: each tooth's viewing ray and apparent size, from its segmented contour — the anchor sits exactly on that ray. Estimated: distance along the ray, from a parabolic dental-arch prior. Assumed: metric scale, from an average 50 mm mouth. Depth is not measured and this is not medical-grade registration.
